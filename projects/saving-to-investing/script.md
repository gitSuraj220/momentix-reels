# Saving → Investing (long video, 16:9, ~5.5 min) — script v1

Working YT title: How to Start Investing in India: From Savings to SIP, Step by Step
Style: notebook paper-collage. Characters: Bunty (only saves) vs Momo (saves + invests).
Our twist (original input): "Gullak vs Khet" analogy, ₹ maths, March 2020 crash example, 2-bucket rule.
Numbers checked: ₹1 L @3% 20y = ₹1.81 L · @12% = ₹9.65 L · SIP ₹5,000 @12% 20y = ₹49.96 L (₹12 L invested).

## Part 1 — Hook (0:00–0:30)
Bunty har mahine savings account mein paisa daalta hai. ₹2,000 yahan, ₹5,000 wahan.
Usse lagta hai, "Main toh bahut responsible hoon."
Ek din app khola — account mein ₹1 lakh pada hai… aur saal bhar ka interest? Sirf ₹3,000.
Aur agar mehngai 5% hai, toh us ₹1 lakh ki value ₹5,000 kam ho gayi.
Matlab Bunty bacha raha tha… par uska paisa chupchaap ghat raha tha.

## Part 2 — Promise (0:30–0:50)
Saving achhi aadat hai. Par sirf saving se wealth nahi banti.
Problem ye hai ki zyada log jaante hi nahi ki saving se investing tak jaana kaise hai — bina dare, bina emergency ka paisa risk kiye.
Toh aaj step by step dekhte hain — saving se investing ka poora raasta.

## Part 3 — Saving vs Investing (0:50–1:30)
Ek line yaad rakho: saving paise ko bachati hai, investing paise ko badhati hai.
Dono ka kaam alag hai.
Maths dekho. ₹1 lakh savings mein 3% pe — 20 saal baad lagbhag ₹1.8 lakh.
Wahi ₹1 lakh agar 12% average return pe badhe — 20 saal baad lagbhag ₹9.6 lakh.
Same paisa, same 20 saal — farak ₹8 lakh ka.
(Nifty 50 ne lambe time mein average around 12% diya hai, par ye guarantee nahi hai.)

## Part 4 — Asli problem: dimaag (1:30–2:10)
Farak maths ka nahi, dimaag ka hai.
Savings account mein paisa hilta nahi. Dikhta hai, safe lagta hai.
Market mein paisa upar-neeche hota hai. Kisi Tuesday dopahar 5% gir gaya — aur dil baith jaata hai.
Isliye Bunty kehta hai, "Mujhe apni savings nahi gawani."
Bilkul sahi baat. Isliye ye shift ek jhatke mein nahi, steps mein hoti hai.

## Part 5 — Step 1: Emergency fund (2:10–2:50)
Step 1 — pehle emergency fund.
Ek bhi rupaya invest karne se pehle, 3 se 6 mahine ka kharcha alag rakho.
Ye paisa invest karne ke liye nahi hai. Job chali gayi, hospital, bike ka repair — uske liye hai.
Log market mein loss pe nahi, zaroorat pe bechte hain. Emergency fund aapke investment ko aapki life se bachata hai.

## Part 6 — Step 2: Do bucket (2:50–3:20)
Step 2 — paise ke do bucket banao.
Bucket 1: suraksha ka paisa — ye hilta nahi.
Bucket 2: badhne wala paisa — isko upar-neeche hone ki permission hai, kyunki iska kaam hai lambe time mein badhna.
Jab dimaag mein ye alag ho jaata hai, darr aadha ho jaata hai.

## Part 7 — Step 3 + 4: Chhota shuru, simple rakho (3:20–4:10)
Step 3 — chhote se shuru karo.
Investing ke liye lakhon nahi chahiye. Kai index fund mein SIP ₹500 se bhi shuru ho jaati hai.
Shuru mein goal profit nahi, aadat hai.
Step 4 — simple cheez se shuru karo.
Beginner seedha F&O, intraday ya crypto mein kood jaate hain — aur wahi sabse zyada jalte hain.
Index fund ek saath top companies mein paisa lagata hai. Kaunsa share jeetega, ye guess nahi karna padta.
Boring hai — par boring hi kaam karta hai.

## Part 8 — SIP ka jaadu (4:10–4:45)
Momo ne har mahine ₹5,000 ki SIP ki. 12% average maan lo.
20 saal baad — lagbhag ₹50 lakh.
Usne khud daale sirf ₹12 lakh. Baaki ₹38 lakh — compounding ne banaye.

## Part 9 — Crash aaya toh? (4:45–5:20)
"Par market crash ho gaya toh?"
Crash normal hai. March 2020 mein Nifty lagbhag 38% gira tha — aur usi saal November tak wapas purane high pe tha.
SIP wale ko girte market mein zyada units sasti milti hain.
Jo saalon mein sochta hai, woh dino ki girawat se nahi darta.

## Part 10 — Summary + CTA (5:20–5:50)
Toh raasta yaad rakho:
Ek — 3 se 6 mahine ka emergency fund.
Do — suraksha aur badhne wala, do bucket.
Teen — chhoti SIP se shuru.
Chaar — simple index fund.
Paanch — SIP band mat karo, compounding ko time do.
Saving gullak mein beej rakhne jaisa hai — safe, par kuch ugta nahi.
Investing khet mein beej bona hai — shuru mein kuch dikhta nahi, par time ke saath ped ban jaata hai.
Aap abhi kis stage pe ho — gullak ya khet? Comment mein batao.
Sirf jaankari ke liye, financial advice nahi.
