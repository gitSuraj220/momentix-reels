# Bloomberg Terminal reel — Suraj's script, flow corrected (v2, 32 lakh per Suraj)
Ref: "The Bloomberg Terminal, explained in 90 seconds" (paper-sketch style). USD/INR ~94.5 (Nov 2026) -> $32,000 ≈ ₹30 lakh.

Ye kaali si screen dekh rahe ho? Iski keemat hai 32 lakh rupaye saal ki, aur phir bhi 3 lakh se zyada log iske paise dete hain.
Kaala background, orange letters, aur ek keyboard jisme peele, hare aur laal buttons hain.
Ispe duniya ka lagbhag har price milta hai. Chahe shares ho, bonds ho, currency ho ya oil, sab kuch.
Ise kehte hain Bloomberg Terminal.
Iski shuruaat hui 1981 mein. Michael Bloomberg ne Salomon Brothers bank chhoda, aur unke paas the kareeb 1 crore dollar.
Ek saal baad, Merrill Lynch ko pehle 20 terminal mile.
Par aaj, isme se bahut saara data doosri jagah sasta milta hai, kuch toh free bhi. LSEG aur FactSet jaise rivals bhi yahi bechte hain.
Toh phir koi 32 lakh rupaye kyun deta hai? Kyunki asli cheez data nahi, ye hai ki is pe kaun-kaun hai.
Har terminal mein ek chat hai, aur usme 3 lakh se zyada clients, banks aur rivals baithe hain.
Shares exchange pe trade hote hain. Par bahut saare bonds aaj bhi ek insaan se doosre insaan ke beech trade hote hain.
Maan lo Sia ko kisi company ka 1 crore dollar ka bond bechna hai. Wo teen dealers ko message karti hai, kuch hi minute mein teen price aa jaate hain. Sabse achha price chunti hai, aur type karti hai, "done".
Terminal ke bina, wo us room mein hoti hi nahi.
Market is pe kitna tika hai, ye pata chala 17 April 2015 ko. Duniya bhar mein terminal ghanton band rahe, aur Britain ko kareeb 3 billion pound ke sarkari bond ki sale aage khiskaani padi.
Bloomberg private company hai, toh iske numbers sirf andaaze hain. Kareeb 14 billion dollar ki kamaai saal ki, zyada tar isi terminal se.
Aam investor ke liye free tools aksar kaafi hote hain.
Kyunki yahan aap data ke paise nahi dete, aap paise dete ho ki us pe kaun hai.
Yahi hai Bloomberg Terminal.
