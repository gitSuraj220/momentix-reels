# Script v1: "Ghar khareedo ya rent pe raho?" (Hinglish, ~35 s, Suraj's voice), 6 Nov 2026
Idea from YouTube Ask Studio. Ending = balanced lens (Claude's call): maths first, then the discipline + security twist.
Characters: Bunty buys (flat on EMI), Momo rents + invests. Our own example and numbers.

## Example maths (all labelled "example" on screen)
Flat Rs 80 lakh. Down payment 20% = Rs 16 L; stamp duty + registration ~7% = Rs 5.6 L. Loan Rs 64 L @ 8% for 20 yrs:
EMI Rs 53,532; total interest ~Rs 64.5 L. Maintenance Rs 3,000/month (+5%/yr).
Rent for the same flat Rs 20,000/month (+5%/yr). Momo invests the Rs 21.6 L upfront + the monthly difference (starts ~Rs 36,500).
After 20 yrs: flat @ 7%/yr = Rs 3.1 Cr. Momo's corpus @ 12% = Rs 5.3 Cr; @ 10% = Rs 3.8 Cr. (Total Momo put in: Rs 82.6 L.)
Ignored for simplicity: tax on the investment gains, home-loan tax benefit, rent-free years after the loan. Verify loan rate/stamp duty/rents before voice.
