# Script v1 — "How Banks Make Money" (Hinglish, ~35 s) — inspired by @financewithafshin (UK) reel DewfPxpolWv; our own Indian data + ladder twist

## Data (checked 4 Nov 2026, SBI as the example bank; simple 1-year interest on ₹1 lakh)
- Savings account: 2.50% p.a. (RateScan, verified 4 Nov 2026; one entry shows 2.75% — say "lagbhag 2.5%") → ₹2,500
- Home loan: 7.25–8.45% (Urban Money, 1 Nov 2026) → use ~8% → ₹8,000
- Personal loan: 10–15% (Urban Money / MyMoneyWay, 2026) → use ~12% → ₹12,000
- Credit card unpaid balance: 3.75% per month = 45% p.a. (BankBazaar, updated 4 Nov 2026) → ₹45,000
- Caveat: banks don't hand your exact notes to a borrower; they also pay staff, CRR, bad loans, etc. "Simple version."
