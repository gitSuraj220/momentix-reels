# Inflation in layman terms — script v2 (Suraj's sketch, 28 Oct 2026)

**Suraj's visual idea:** a 3D samosa rotating → a village shop where a small figure buys samosas → ₹100 = 10 samose → same samosa later ₹20 → ₹100 = only 5 → "wahi samosa… phir ₹10 se ₹20 kaise? Ise kehte hain inflation."

**Fixes:** (1) ₹10→₹20 in ONE year = 100% inflation (wrong; comments will catch it) → use **10 saal**: ~7%/yr doubles a price in ~10 years (rule of 72), close to India's food inflation 7.74% (Sep 2026). (2) "wahi rate" → "wahi size, wahi swaad". (3) Add a 1-line *why* and a twist ending so people share.
