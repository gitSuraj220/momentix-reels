Part 1 · Video summary

Poori strategy, 4 steps mein

01
Book value se neeche wali company Book value = company apna sab kuch bech de, saara karz chuka de, phir jo bache. Usko total shares se divide karo = book value per share. Agar share ka price book value se kam hai, matlab company apni asli keemat se bhi sasti mil rahi hai. Price ÷ Book value (P/B) < 1.
02
Last quarter = 3 saal ka best Sasta hona kaafi nahi; kai companies isliye sasti hain kyunki business doob raha hai. Isliye sirf wo rakho jinka latest quarter ka profit pichhle 12 quarters (3 saal) mein sabse zyada ho. Sasti bhi, aur business peak pe: ye combo rare hai.
03
Human test: kya ye tikegi? Ye step koi software nahi karega. Ek sawaal: kya ye company agle 3–4 saal survive karegi aur growth maintain kar paayegi? Hype ya sentiment pe chalne wali company ho to doubt rakho. Sawaal "kitna badhega" nahi, sawaal "kya ye tikega".
04
Kab khareedna, kab bechna Buy: jab saari conditions tick ho jaayein aur price book value se neeche ho.
Rule 1: buy ke 6 mahine ke andar price book value tak pahuncha (ya cross kiya) → profit book.
Rule 2: 6 mahine mein nahi pahuncha → hold, aur bech do jab price book value ka double ho (BV ₹100 → target ₹200).
Pro tip: book value har 6 mahine update hoti hai (naya balance sheet), to target bhi har 6 mahine update karo.
Value trap: kuch companies saalon tak book value se neeche hi padi rehti hain. Step 3 isi se bachata hai.

Part 2 · Manually (free)

Step 1 + 2: list banao, best quarter check karo

Aapko bas ek free account chahiye: screener.in (Indian stocks ka free data website).

Step 1 · P/B < 1 wali list

1
screener.in kholo → free account banao (Sign up).
2
Upar Screens → Create new screen dabao.
3
Query box mein ye copy-paste karo, phir Run this query:
Price to book value < 1 AND Market Capitalization > 500
Market Capitalization > 500 (₹ crore) bahut chhoti, kam trade hone wali companies hata deta hai.
4
Jo list aayi, wahi aapki Step 1 list hai. Isme se ek-ek company kholo.

Step 2 · Best quarter check (har company ke liye)

1
Company page pe neeche scroll karo → Quarterly Results section.
2
Net Profit wali row dekho. Sabse right wala number = latest quarter.
3
Check karo: latest quarter ka profit, uske pehle ke 12 quarters (3 saal) mein sabse zyada hai? Haan → company rakho. Nahi → hata do.
Example (dummy numbers)
Pichhle 12 quarters ka net profit (₹ cr): 40, 42, 38, 45, 47, 44, 50, 52, 49, 55, 53, 68 → latest 68 sabse zyada hai ✓ Company list mein rahegi.
Dhyaan do: agar latest quarter mein "Other income" bahut zyada hai, to profit ek baar ki kamaai se bhi ho sakta hai. Aise case mein savdhaan raho.

Part 2 · Manually (free)

Step 3 + 4: dimaag lagao, exit plan likho

Step 3 · Human test checklist

Shortlist ki har company ke liye ye 6 sawaal khud se pucho (Screener page, annual report aur BSE/NSE announcements se):

  • Business samjha? Company kya bechti hai, aur 3–4 saal baad bhi log ye khareedenge?
  • Karz: Debt to equity bahut zyada to nahi? Interest aaram se bhar pa rahi hai?
  • Profit asli hai? Profit ke saath cash flow from operations bhi positive hai?
  • Promoter: promoter holding gir to nahi rahi, shares pledge to nahi hain?
  • Hype check: price sirf news, sentiment ya hype se to nahi bhaga?
  • Final: "Kya ye tikegi?" Jawab pakka haan nahi → company chhod do.

Step 4 · Exit plan (buy karte hi likh lo)

1
Company page ke upar Book Value (per share) note karo. Ye aapka Target 1 hai.
2
Book value × 2 = Target 2.
3
Buy date + 6 mahine = Rule 1 ki last date. Calendar mein reminder laga do.
4
Har 6 mahine (naya half-yearly balance sheet aane pe) book value dobara dekho aur dono targets update karo.

Part 3 · AI prompts

Yahi kaam AI se, 2 minute mein

Koi bhi AI chatbot chalega (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini). Screener.in se company ka data copy karo, neeche wala prompt paste karo, aur [ ] waali jagah data bhar do.

Prompt 1 · Ek company ka poora check (Steps 1, 2, 4)

Tum ek careful stock-data assistant ho. Sirf calculation aur data check karo. Koi buy/sell recommendation mat do. Company: [naam] Current price: ₹[price] Book value per share: ₹[book value] Quarterly net profit, purane se naye (₹ cr, 13 quarters): [paste karo] Meri buy date (agar khareeda hai): [DD-MM-YYYY] Mujhe ek simple table mein batao: 1) P/B = price ÷ book value. P/B 1 se kam hai? (Haan/Nahi) 2) Latest quarter ka profit, pichhle 12 quarters mein sabse zyada hai? (Haan/Nahi + 3 saal ka max number) 3) Koi red flag jo data mein dikhe (jaise ek quarter mein achanak bahut bada jump, jo one-time income ho sakta hai) 4) Exit plan: Target 1 = book value, Target 2 = 2 x book value, Rule 1 ki last date = buy date + 6 mahine 5) Agla review date: buy date + 6 mahine (naya book value check) Agar koi data missing hai to guess mat karo, mujhse puchho.

Prompt 2 · Poori list ek saath (Step 2)

Neeche kuch companies ka quarterly Net Profit data hai (₹ cr, purane se naye). Har company ke liye batao latest quarter pichhle 12 quarters mein sabse zyada hai ya nahi. Table do: Company | Latest | 3-saal max | Best? | Red flag. Sirf data check, koi advice nahi. [yahan 5-10 companies ka data paste karo]

Part 3 · AI prompts

Prompt 3 · Human test (AI sawaal puchhega, faisla aap karoge)

Company: [naam]. Main khud decide karunga ki ye company agle 3-4 saal survive aur grow karegi ya nahi. Mujhe final answer mat do. Mujhse ek-ek karke ye sawaal pucho aur har sawaal ke saath batao ki main ye data kahan check karun (annual report, BSE/NSE filing, Screener page): 1) Business kya bechta hai, aur iski demand kitni stable hai? 2) Karz kitna hai, interest aaram se bhar pa rahi hai? 3) Profit ke saath operating cash flow bhi positive hai? 4) Promoter holding ya pledge mein koi badlaav? 5) Kya price sirf hype ya news se bhaga hai? 6) Sector ke agle 5-10 saal ke bade risk kya hain? Mere jawab sunke sirf summary do: kaunse points strong, kaunse weak.

Prompt 4 · Har 6 mahine ka update

Mera holding: [company], buy price ₹[x], buy date [date]. Purana book value ₹[old], naya book value ₹[new] (latest balance sheet). Naye targets calculate karo: Target 1 = naya book value, Target 2 = 2 x naya book value. Current price ₹[price] ke hisaab se batao kaunsa rule abhi apply hota hai. Sirf calculation, koi advice nahi.
Part 4 · Zaroori baatein
  • Value trap: har sasta share achha nahi hota. Step 3 kabhi skip mat karna.
  • AI galat ho sakta hai: har number company ki official filing ya Screener page se khud verify karo.
  • Ek hi stock mein saara paisa nahi. Utna hi lagao jitna risk utha sako.
