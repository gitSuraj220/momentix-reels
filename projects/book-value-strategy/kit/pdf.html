Part A · Screener.in (free)

Step 1: P/B < 1 wali list banao

1
screener.in pe free account banao → upar Screens → Create new screen.
2
Query box mein ye likho aur Run this query dabao:
Price to book value < 1 AND Market Capitalization > 500
Market cap > 500 (₹ crore) bahut chhoti, illiquid companies hata deta hai. Apne hisaab se badal sakte ho.
3
Result table mein Edit columns se ye columns add karo: Price to book value, Net profit (latest quarter), Sales growth, Debt to equity. Phir poori table select karke copy karo.

Step 2: AI se best-quarter filter

Har shortlisted company ke Screener page pe Quarterly Results section hota hai (12+ quarters). Uski Net Profit row copy karke AI ko do:

Tum ek careful data assistant ho. Main neeche kuch companies ka quarterly Net Profit data (₹ crore, purane se naya) de raha hoon. Har company ke liye check karo: 1) Latest quarter ka net profit, pichhle 12 quarters (3 saal) ka sabse zyada hai ya nahi? (Haan / Nahi) 2) Kya latest profit kisi one-time gain ki wajah se ho sakta hai? (agar data se lagta hai to flag karo) Result ek table mein do: Company | Latest qtr | 3-saal ka max | Best? | Flag Koi buy/sell recommendation mat do, sirf data check karo. [yahan data paste karo]
Tip: ek baar mein 5–10 companies ka data do. Jo "Best = Haan" aaye, wahi Step 3 ke liye shortlist hai.

Part B · Human test + exit tracker

Step 3: Human test (AI sirf sawaal puchhega)

Company: [naam]. Main khud decide karunga ki ye company agle 3–4 saal survive aur grow karegi ya nahi. Mujhe answer mat do, mujhse sahi sawaal pucho: - business kya bechta hai, demand kitni stable hai - karz (debt) kitna hai, interest bhar pa rahi hai ya nahi - promoter holding / pledge me koi badlaav - result sentiment ya one-time cheez se to nahi aaya - sector ke 10 saal ke risk Har sawaal ke saath batao main ye data kahan check karun (annual report, BSE filing, Screener page).

Step 4: Exit tracker

Meri buy details: Company [naam], buy date [DD-MM-YYYY], buy price ₹[x], book value per share ₹[y] (latest balance sheet). Ek simple table banao: - Rule 1 window: buy date se 6 mahine tak ki last date - Rule 1 target: ₹[y] (book value) - Rule 2 target: ₹[2y] (2x book value), agar 6 mahine mein Rule 1 hit nahi hua - Next update dates: har 6 mahine (naye balance sheet ke baad book value badlo) Sirf calculation karo, koi advice nahi.
Value trap se bacho: kuch companies saalon tak book value se neeche rehti hain. Isiliye Step 3 skip mat karna.
