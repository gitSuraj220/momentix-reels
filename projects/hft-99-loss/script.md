## 1:10 — High Frequency Trading
[S] Ise kehte hain High Frequency Trading.
[S] Jab bhi aap trading kar rahe hote ho aur Buy ka button dabate ho, aapke opposite mein ye giants apni algorithm se trade kar rahe hote hain.
[+] HFT yaani computer programs, jo ek second ke bahut chhote hisson mein orders daalte aur nikaalte hain — insaan ki palak jhapakne se kahin tez.
[+] Inke servers exchange ke data centre ke andar ya bilkul paas rakhe jaate hain. Isse bolte hain co-location — taaki order exchange tak pahunchne mein microseconds bhi na lagein.

## 2:30 — Speed ki race
[S] Inhone apne system ko fast banane ke liye samundar mein khud ki ek cable daal rakhi hai, servers wahan place kar rakhe hain jahan stock market trade hota hai — yaani nano seconds ka bhi delay inhe manzoor nahi.
[+] Ek chhota example: jab aap button dabate ho, aapka order phone → broker → exchange jaata hai. Wahi kaam ye firms apne servers se exchange ke andar baithe-baithe kar leti hain.

## 3:30 — Paisa kitna
[S] Ek report ke hisaab se ek saal mein in firms ne 7 billion dollar ka profit kamaya — same utna hi paisa Indian trader ne loss mein gawaya.
[+] Real case: Jul 2025 mein SEBI ne Jane Street par rok lagayi aur ₹4,844 crore ka disgorgement order kiya. SEBI ka allegation tha ki firm ne index manipulation se ₹36,500 crore ke aas-paas gross profit kamaya. Ye SEBI ke allegations hain — firm ne aarop nahi maane.
[+] Aur SEBI ki FY26 study: retail F&O traders ka total loss ₹91,685 crore, average loss ₹1.17 lakh per trader.
