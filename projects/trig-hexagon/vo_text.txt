Boards mein top karna hai, par maths panga le rahi hai? Bro, ye sattaees trig formulas ratne ki zarurat hi nahi. Bas ek hexagon banao. Left mein upar se: sine, tan, sec. Right mein unke co wale: cos, cot, cosec. Beech mein one. Bas, setup done. Rule one: aamne-saamne multiply karo, answer hamesha one. Sine into cosec, cos into sec, tan into cot. Rule two: ghadi ki direction mein chalo. Tan equals sine by cos. Ulta chalo, cot equals cos by sine. Rule three: koi bhi kona equals uske do padosi multiply. Tan equals sine into sec. Rule four: neeche ke teen triangles. Upar ke do ka square jodo, neeche wala mil jayega. Sine square plus cos square equals one. Literally cheat code hai ye! Poori trick sheet chahiye?
