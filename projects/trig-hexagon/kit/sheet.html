27 Trig Formulas, 1 Hexagon

Ratna band. Pehle hexagon banao, phir padho. Class 10 · 11 · 12 · JEE
TRICK SHEET

Kaise banayein (10 second)

  1. Left, upar se neeche: sin, tan, sec
  2. Right, unke "co" wale: cos, cot, cosec
  3. Beech mein 1
  4. Neeche ke 3 triangles shade karo
Exam tip: rough sheet pe sabse pehle ye hexagon bana lo. Koi formula bhoolo toh 4 rules se padh lo.

RULE 1 · Aamne-saamne = 1 6

Opposite corners (centre se hoke) multiply = 1, toh har ek doosre ka 1/ hai.

  • sin = 1/cosec
  • cosec = 1/sin
  • cos = 1/sec
  • sec = 1/cos
  • tan = 1/cot
  • cot = 1/tan

RULE 2 · Ghadi ki direction 12

Koi bhi kona = agla kona ÷ uske baad wala. Clockwise aur anticlockwise, dono.

  • tan = sin/cos
  • sin = cos/cot
  • cos = cot/cosec
  • cot = cosec/sec
  • cosec = sec/tan
  • sec = tan/sin
  • tan = sec/cosec
  • sec = cosec/cot
  • cosec = cot/cos
  • cot = cos/sin
  • cos = sin/tan
  • sin = tan/sec

RULE 3 · Do padosi multiply 6

Koi bhi kona = uske dono padosi (side wale corners) ka product.

  • tan = sin × sec
  • sin = tan × cos
  • cos = sin × cot
  • cot = cos × cosec
  • cosec = cot × sec
  • sec = cosec × tan

RULE 4 · Triangles 3

Har shaded triangle mein: upar ke do ka square jodo = neeche wale ka square.

  • sin2 + cos2 = 1
  • tan2 + 1 = sec2
  • 1 + cot2 = cosec2

Check karke dekho (ek example)

Rule 2 se: sin = cos ÷ cot. Ab cot = cos/sin daalo: cos ÷ (cos/sin) = cos × (sin/cos) = sin ✓

Rule 4 se: sin2 + cos2 = 1 ko cos2 se divide karo → tan2 + 1 = sec2. sin2 se divide karo → 1 + cot2 = cosec2.
