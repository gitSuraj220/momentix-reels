## Math check (verified)
Layout: left top->bottom sin, tan, sec; right top->bottom cos, cot, cosec; 1 in centre.
- Reciprocal (opposite through centre): sin*cosec=1, cos*sec=1, tan*cot=1 (6 identities incl. both forms)
- Quotient (corner = next / one after, either direction): tan=sin/cos, cot=cos/sin, sin=cos/cot ... (12)
- Product (corner = two neighbours multiplied): tan=sin*sec, cos=sin*cot, sec=tan*cosec ... (6)
- Pythagorean (3 downward triangles, top two squared = bottom): sin^2+cos^2=1, tan^2+1=sec^2, 1+cot^2=cosec^2 (3)
Total 6+12+6+3 = 27. All checked algebraically.
