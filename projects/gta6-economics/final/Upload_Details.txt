GTA 6 is finally here after 13 years. 🎮
It cost an estimated ₹14,000 crore to make… and sells for just ₹7,500. So how does it ever pay off?

The store keeps about 30% of every copy, so roughly ₹5,250 reaches the company. That's about 2.7 crore copies just to pay back the build. GTA 5 has already sold over 23 crore. But the real twist: of every ₹100 Take-Two earns, ₹81 comes from players spending inside games they already own: cars, skins, houses. GTA 5 is 13 years old and still earning.

The game is the ticket. The money is inside. 💸
