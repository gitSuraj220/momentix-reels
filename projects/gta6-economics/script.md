Facts (verified 19 Nov): GTA 6 launched 19 Nov 2026 (PS5, Xbox Series X|S), $80 US / ₹5,999–7,499 India; build cost $1–1.5B = analyst estimate (not published);

3. Har copy pe store 30% kaat leta hai. Company ke haath aate hain sirf ₹5,250.
4. Toh kharcha nikalne ke liye chahiye 2.7 crore copies. Sounds crazy?
5. Par GTA 5 ki toh 23 crore se zyada copies bik chuki hain.
6. Aur asli twist ye hai: GTA ki company game bechke kam, aur game ke andar ki shopping se zyada kamaati hai.
7. Har ₹100 ki kamai mein se ₹81 aate hain un cheezon se jo log game ke andar khareedte hain: gaadiyan, skins, ghar.
8. GTA 5 13 saal purana hai, aur aaj bhi paise kama raha hai.
