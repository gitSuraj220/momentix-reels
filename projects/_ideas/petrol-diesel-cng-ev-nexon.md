# IDEA (saved 31 Oct 2026, make later): Petrol vs Diesel vs CNG vs EV — same car (Tata Nexon)

**Reference reel:** https://www.instagram.com/reel/Der2VhbvtcV/ (@billeniumworld, "Same Car, Shocking Math!")

## Verified facts
- Running cost per km (Autocar India, 12 Sep 2026, real-world test):
  - Turbo-petrol DCA ₹9.46/km (7.7 kpl city / 13.9 highway)
  - Diesel MT ₹5.30/km (17.95 kpl)
  - CNG ₹4.28/km (15.1 km/kg city, ~24 highway)
  - EV 45 kWh home charging (₹8/unit) ₹1.03/km; public DC fast charging (₹24–28/unit) ₹3.34/km
- Top EV (Empowered+ A 45) is ₹2.65 lakh dearer than top petrol DCA; gap recovered in ~32,000 km (Autocar).
- Diesel ≈ ₹1 lakh premium over equivalent turbo-petrol.
- Ex-showroom ranges: petrol ₹7.40–13.90 L, CNG ₹8.35–13.60 L, diesel ₹9.15–14.40 L, EV ₹12.49–17.49 L.
- Fuel prices 31 Oct 2026 (Punjab Kesari): Delhi petrol ₹102.12, diesel ₹95.20, CNG ₹86.98/kg; Mumbai petrol ₹111.21; Hyderabad petrol ₹116.15.
