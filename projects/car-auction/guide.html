BANK AUCTION SE CAR KAISE KHAREEDEIN

1 page cheat sheet · Process, checklist aur chhupe kharche · Sirf jaankari ke liye

1Auction kahan milte hain

  • Foreclosure India
  • eAuction India
  • Bankeauctions.com
  • Banks ki apni websites (auction / repossessed vehicles section)

Tip: sirf jaane-maane portal ya bank ki official site use karo. Kisi agent ko "pakki deal" ke naam pe advance mat do.

2Auction notice mein ye padho

  • Reserve price (sabse kam bid)
  • EMD amount aur jama karne ki last date
  • Inspection ki date aur yard ka address
  • Bid jeetne ke baad payment ki deadline
  • "As-is-where-is" jaisi terms

3Process: 3 step + kaagaz

Step 1 · Register + KYCPortal pe account banao. PAN, Aadhaar, address proof aur bank details lagte hain.
Step 2 · InspectionYard jaake gaadi khud dekho; chaho toh mechanic le jao. Aam taur pe test drive nahi milti.
Step 3 · EMD + BidEMD jama karke online bid lagao. Haar gaye toh EMD wapas. Jeete toh baaki paisa deadline ke andar (aksar 7–15 din).

Jeetne ke baad: Sale certificate/letter, bank ka NOC aur Form 35 (loan/hypothecation hatane ke liye) lo, phir RTO mein RC apne naam transfer karao.

4Inspection checklist

  • Engine: awaaz, dhuan, oil leak
  • Chassis aur engine number RC se match
  • Tyres, brakes, suspension
  • Body: accident, rust, paint mismatch
  • Odometer vs gaadi ki haalat
  • Pending challan: Parivahan / e-challan site pe check

5Chhupe kharche

  • Repair aur service
  • RTO transfer fee
  • Naya insurance
  • Towing / transport
  • Yard parking charges (agar hon)
  • Purane challan ya road tax dues

Bid se pehle ye sab jod ke apna max budget fix karo.

⚠️ Dhyan rakho

  • Gaadi "jaisi hai, jahan hai" bikti hai: warranty ya return nahi.
  • Payment deadline miss hui toh EMD jabt ho sakta hai. Paisa pehle se ready rakho.
  • Har auction ke rules alag hote hain; bid se pehle us notice ki terms zaroor padho.
