## 1. SIP kya hai?
SIP ka matlab hai Systematic Investment Plan. Aasaan bhasha mein: har mahine ek tay date pe, ek tay paisa, apne aap aapke bank account se kat ke stock market mein lag jaata hai.

SIP un investors ke liye hai jo har mahine thoda-thoda invest karna chahte hain. SIP ka ek bada fayda hai. Jab market neeche hota hai, toh aapki SIP mein zyada units milti hain. Jab market upar hota hai, toh kam units milti hain. Aisa karne se long term mein aapki investment achha return de sakti hai.

Par aapko toh pata nahi hai ki paise kahan lagane hain. Toh ab baat aati hai mutual fund ki.

## 2. Mutual fund
Mutual fund ko ek badi tokri samjho. Hazaaron logon ka paisa is tokri mein jaata hai. Ek expert, jise fund manager kehte hain, is paise ko alag-alag companies ke shares mein lagata hai. Badle mein aapko units milti hain.

Ab mutual fund bhi apne risk ke hisaab se mainly 3 categories mein bante hue hain: Large cap, Mid cap aur Small cap.

## 3. Large cap, Mid cap, Small cap
India ki 100 sabse badi companies Large cap hain.
101 se 250 number tak ki companies Mid cap hain.
251 ke baad ki saari companies Small cap hain.

Ise example se samjho:
Large cap ek badi SUV jaisa hai. Speed thodi kam hai, par safar aaraam ka hai aur jhatke kam lagte hain.
Mid cap ek sedan jaisa hai. Speed bhi theek hai aur jhatke bhi beech ke hain.
Small cap ek sports bike jaisa hai. Bahut tez bhaag sakti hai, par girne ka risk bhi sabse zyada hai.

Jab market girta hai, toh small cap aksar sabse zyada girte hain, aur bahut log isi waqt ghabra ke SIP band kar dete hain.

Toh aapke liye kaunsa sahi hai? Agar utaar-chadhaav kam chahiye, toh large cap. Aur agar zyada utaar-chadhaav jhel sakte ho, toh mid cap ya small cap.

## 4. App pe SIP kaise shuru karein: 6 steps
Har app thoda alag dikhta hai, par steps lagbhag same hote hain.
Step 1, KYC: PAN card, Aadhaar aur bank account ki details daalo. KYC ek hi baar hota hai, phir har fund mein chalega.
Step 2, fund chuno: category dekho — Large cap, Mid cap ya Small cap.
Step 3: "SIP" pe click karo, "One-time" pe nahi. Phir amount daalo, jaise ₹1,000.
Step 4, date chuno: salary aane ke 2-3 din baad ki date rakho, taaki account mein paisa ho.
Step 5, autopay lagao: UPI Autopay ya bank mandate approve karo. Ek baar approve kar diya, toh har mahine paisa apne aap katega.
Step 6, confirm karo: pehli kisht katne ke kuch din baad units aapke account mein dikhne lagengi.
Bas! Aapki SIP chalu ho gayi.

## 5. Baad mein kaam aane wali baatein
Paise ki tangi hai, toh bahut se apps mein SIP kuch mahino ke liye "pause" karne ka option hota hai.
SIP "cancel" karne se aapka jama paisa nahi nikalta. Bas aage ki kisht rukti hai.
Salary badhi, toh "Step-up" se har saal SIP ka amount badha sakte ho.
Kai funds mein 1 saal se pehle paisa nikalne pe thodi fees lagti hai, jise exit load kehte hain. Ye aksar 1% ke aas-paas hoti hai.

## 6. Ye 4 galtiyan mat karna
1. Market gira aur SIP band kar di, jabki girte market mein hi zyada units milti hain.
2. Ek saath 8-10 fund le liye. Aksar sabke andar same companies hoti hain.
3. Sirf pichhle saal ka return dekh ke fund chun liya.
4. Emergency ke liye paisa alag rakhe bina SIP shuru kar di.
