# SIP kaise shuru karein + Large / Mid / Small cap — long video script v1 (~7–8 min)

Narrator: Amrut. Visuals: animation (no screen recording) — generic "MF app" mockup, no real brand/fund names.

## HOOK
September 2026 mein, sirf ek mahine mein, Indians ne SIP ke through ₹32,490 crore mutual funds mein daale. Ye ab tak ka record hai.
Par jab aap app kholte ho, saamne teen shabd aate hain: Large cap, Mid cap, Small cap. Aur bahut log yahin atak jaate hain.
Aaj is video mein teen cheezein: SIP hoti kya hai, in teen tarah ke funds mein farq kya hai, aur app pe step-by-step SIP set kaise karte hain.

## 1. SIP kya hai
SIP matlab Systematic Investment Plan. Simple bhasha mein: har mahine ek fixed date pe, ek fixed amount, apne aap aapke bank se kat ke mutual fund mein chala jaata hai.
Jab market neeche hota hai, toh usi ₹1,000 mein zyada units milti hain. Jab upar hota hai, toh kam. Isse kehte hain rupee cost averaging — lambe time mein aapki average khareed keemat smooth ho jaati hai.
Aur kai funds mein SIP ₹100 ya ₹500 se bhi shuru ho jaati hai.

## 2. Mutual fund kaam kaise karta hai
Mutual fund ek bada basket hai. Hazaaron logon ka paisa ek jagah judta hai, aur ek fund manager us paise ko alag-alag companies ke shares mein lagata hai. Badle mein aapko milti hain units.

## 3. Large, Mid, Small cap
India ki listed companies ko unke size, matlab market cap, ke hisaab se line mein khada karo.
- Top 100 companies: Large cap. Desh ki sabse badi, jaani-maani companies.
- 101 se 250 number tak: Mid cap. Badhti hui companies.
- 251 ke baad: Small cap. Chhoti companies.
Ye list AMFI har 6 mahine mein update karta hai.
SEBI ke niyam se, large cap fund ko kam se kam 80% paisa top 100 companies mein lagana padta hai. Mid cap fund ko kam se kam 65% mid caps mein, aur small cap fund ko kam se kam 65% small caps mein.

Ise teen gaadiyon se samjho:
- Large cap = badi SUV. Dheere chalti hai, par jhatke kam.
- Mid cap = sedan. Speed bhi, jhatke bhi — dono beech ke.
- Small cap = sports bike. Tez bhaag sakti hai, par girne ka risk bhi sabse zyada.
Market girta hai toh small cap aksar sabse zyada girte hain. Aur bahut log isi giraavat mein ghabra ke SIP band kar dete hain.

Kaunsa kiske liye? Koi ek "best" nahi hai. Do sawaal poochho:
1. Paisa kab chahiye? Jitna lamba time, utna zyada utaar-chadhaav jhel sakte ho.
2. Giraavat dekh ke aap kitna shaant reh sakte ho?
Aapke liye kya sahi hai, ye kisi SEBI-registered advisor se poochhna better hai.

## 4. App pe SIP setup — 6 steps
Har app thoda alag dikhta hai, par steps lagbhag same hote hain.
- Step 1 — KYC: PAN, Aadhaar aur bank account. Ek baar KYC ho gaya, toh har fund mein chalega.
- Step 2 — Fund chuno: category dekho — Large, Mid ya Small cap. Do cheezein zaroor dekho: plan "Direct" hai ya "Regular", aur expense ratio. Direct plan mein commission nahi hota, isliye expense ratio kam hota hai.
- Step 3 — "SIP" chuno, "One-time" nahi. Amount daalo, jaise ₹1,000.
- Step 4 — SIP date: salary ke 2–3 din baad ki date rakho, taaki bank mein paisa ho.
- Step 5 — Autopay: UPI Autopay ya bank mandate (e-NACH). Ek baar approve kiya, toh har mahine apne aap katega.
- Step 6 — Confirm: pehli installment katne ke kuch din mein units aapke account mein dikhne lagti hain.
Bas. SIP chalu.

## 5. Pause, cancel, step-up, nikalna
- Paise ki tangi? Bahut se apps aur fund houses mein SIP kuch mahino ke liye pause karne ka option hota hai.
- "Cancel SIP" karne se lagaya hua paisa nahi nikalta — bas aage ki installments rukti hain.
- Salary badhi? Step-up se har saal SIP amount badha sakte ho.
- Paisa nikalna ho toh dhyaan: kai equity funds mein 1 saal se pehle nikalne pe exit load lagta hai, aksar 1% ke aas-paas. Fund ka document check karo.

## 6. Tax (simple)
1 saal se pehle bechne pe faayde par 20% tax lagta hai. 1 saal ke baad, ek saal mein ₹1.25 lakh tak ka faayda tax-free, uske upar 12.5%. Ye niyam Budget 2026 ke baad bhi same hain. Aur SIP ki har installment ka 1 saal alag se gina jaata hai.

## 7. 4 galtiyan
1. Market gira toh SIP band kar di — jabki giraavat mein hi zyada units milti hain.
2. Ek saath 8–10 fund le liye — aksar andar same companies repeat hoti hain.
3. Sirf pichhle saal ka return dekh ke fund chuna.
4. Emergency fund ke bina SIP shuru ki — phir zaroorat padi toh giraavat mein bechna pada.

## OUTRO
Recap: SIP = har mahine fixed paisa, automatically. Large cap — zyada stable, mid cap — beech mein, small cap — sabse zyada utaar-chadhaav. Aur setup sirf 6 step ka.
