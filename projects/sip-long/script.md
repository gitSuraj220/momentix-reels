## HOOK (Suraj's words, flow corrected)
SIP ke baare mein aapne bahut suna hoga. Par ho sakta hai aap aaj tak SIP chalu nahi kar paaye, ya phir aapko SIP samajh hi nahi aayi. Toh ye video aapke liye hai. Is video ko end tak dekhne ke baad SIP ko lekar aapka koi doubt nahi rahega, aur aap aaraam se apni SIP shuru kar paayenge.

## 1. SIP kya hai?
SIP ka matlab hai Systematic Investment Plan.
Aasaan bhasha mein: har mahine ek tay date pe, ek tay paisa, apne aap aapke bank account se kat ke mutual fund mein chala jaata hai.
Jaise har mahine ki EMI apne aap kat-ti hai, waise hi. Bas yahan paisa aapke hi naam pe jama hota hai.

SIP ka ek bada fayda hai.
Jab market neeche hota hai, toh aapke ₹1,000 mein zyada units milti hain.
Jab market upar hota hai, toh kam units milti hain.
Isse lambe time mein aapki average keemat barabar ho jaati hai. Isliye aapko market ka sahi time dhoondhne ki tension nahi leni padti.

Aur kai funds mein SIP sirf ₹100 ya ₹500 se bhi shuru ho jaati hai.

## 2. Mutual fund kya hota hai?
Mutual fund ko ek badi tokri samjho.
Hazaaron logon ka paisa is tokri mein judta hai.
Ek expert, jise fund manager kehte hain, is paise ko alag-alag companies ke shares mein lagata hai.
Badle mein aapko milti hain units. Companies achha karti hain toh units ki keemat badhti hai, aur market girta hai toh ghat bhi sakti hai.

## 3. Large cap, Mid cap, Small cap
Ab sabse zaroori baat.
Share market ki saari companies ko unke size ke hisaab se ek line mein khada karo.
- Pehli 100 sabse badi companies — ye hain Large cap.
- 101 se 250 number tak ki companies — ye hain Mid cap.
- 251 ke baad ki saari companies — ye hain Small cap.

Ye list har 6 mahine mein update hoti hai.
Aur niyam ye hai ki large cap fund ko apna kam se kam 80% paisa top 100 companies mein lagana padta hai. Mid cap aur small cap fund ko kam se kam 65% paisa apni-apni category mein lagana padta hai.

Ise teen gaadiyon se samjho:
- Large cap ek badi SUV jaisa hai. Speed thodi kam, par safar aaraam ka — jhatke kam.
- Mid cap ek sedan jaisa hai. Speed bhi theek, jhatke bhi beech ke.
- Small cap ek sports bike jaisa hai. Bahut tez bhaag sakti hai, par girne ka risk bhi sabse zyada.

Jab market girta hai, toh small cap aksar sabse zyada girte hain.
Aur bahut log isi waqt ghabra ke SIP band kar dete hain.

Toh aapke liye kaunsa sahi hai? Koi ek "best" fund nahi hota. Bas khud se do sawaal poochho:
1. Ye paisa mujhe kab chahiye? Jitna lamba time, utna zyada utaar-chadhaav aap jhel sakte ho.
2. Market girega toh kya main shaant reh paunga?
Agar pakka nahi pata, toh kisi SEBI-registered advisor se salah lena achha hai.

## 4. App pe SIP kaise shuru karein — 6 steps
Har app thoda alag dikhta hai, par steps lagbhag same hote hain.

Step 1 — KYC.
PAN card, Aadhaar aur bank account ki details daalo. KYC ek hi baar hota hai, phir har fund mein chalega.

Step 2 — Fund chuno.
Category dekho: Large cap, Mid cap ya Small cap.
Aur do cheezein zaroor check karo:
Pehli, plan "Direct" hai ya "Regular". Direct plan mein beech mein koi commission nahi hota.
Doosri, "expense ratio" — ye fund ki saalana fees hai. Jitni kam, utna achha.

Step 3 — "SIP" pe click karo, "One-time" pe nahi.
Amount daalo, jaise ₹1,000.

Step 4 — Date chuno.
Salary aane ke 2–3 din baad ki date rakho, taaki account mein paisa ho.

Step 5 — Autopay lagao.
UPI Autopay ya bank mandate approve karo. Ek baar approve kiya, phir har mahine paisa apne aap katega.

Step 6 — Confirm karo.
Pehli kisht katne ke kuch din baad units aapke account mein dikhne lagengi.

Bas! Aapki SIP chalu ho gayi.

## 5. Baad mein kaam aane wali baatein
- Paise ki tangi hai? Bahut se apps mein SIP kuch mahino ke liye "pause" karne ka option hota hai.
- SIP "cancel" karne se aapka jama paisa nahi nikalta. Bas aage ki kisht rukti hai.
- Salary badhi? "Step-up" se har saal SIP ka amount badha sakte ho.
- Paisa nikalna hai? Kai funds mein 1 saal se pehle nikalne pe thodi fees lagti hai, jise exit load kehte hain — aksar 1% ke aas-paas.

## 6. Tax — seedhi baat
- 1 saal se pehle beche, toh munafe pe 20% tax.
- 1 saal ke baad beche, toh saal mein ₹1.25 lakh tak ka munafa tax-free. Uske upar 12.5% tax.
- SIP mein har kisht ka 1 saal alag se gina jaata hai.

## 7. Ye 4 galtiyan mat karna
1. Market gira aur SIP band kar di. Jabki girte market mein hi zyada units milti hain.
2. Ek saath 8–10 fund le liye. Aksar sabke andar same companies hoti hain.
3. Sirf pichhle saal ka return dekh ke fund chuna.
4. Emergency ke liye paisa alag rakhe bina SIP shuru kar di.
