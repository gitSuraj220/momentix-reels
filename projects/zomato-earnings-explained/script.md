**Idea:** Eternal (Zomato + Blinkit) Q2 FY27, Jul–Sep 2026. Revenue up 78%, but costs are higher than revenue. Profit is only 52 paise per ₹100, and it doesn't come from the business itself.
**Rules:** no logos (names in text/voice only), sources on screen, "Sirf jaankari ke liye" in description + caption.

## Facts (results 21 Oct 2026)
- Revenue from operations ₹24,138 cr (+78% YoY, from ₹13,590 cr). Outlook Business / NiftyTrader.
- Total expenses ₹24,166 cr, i.e. ₹28 cr more than revenue. NiftyTrader.
- Net profit ₹125 cr (+92% from ₹65 cr). Estimates were ₹250 cr (CNBC-TV18) and ₹271 cr (Reuters avg). Shares fell about 3.5% on 21 Oct.
- Per ₹100 of revenue: profit ≈ ₹0.52 (125 / 24,138), costs ≈ ₹100.12.
- Order value: Blinkit ₹20,940 cr vs food delivery ₹11,445 cr. Blinkit +308 stores, 2,751 total. Cash ₹16,475 cr. Investywise (shareholder letter).
- Ad & promotion spend ₹1,138 cr (+41%). exchange4media.
- Why profit > 0 despite expenses > revenue: other income (e.g. interest/returns on its cash and investments). Phrase it as "jaise".
