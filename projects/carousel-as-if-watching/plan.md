# Carousel: "As if…" — Momo life habits (IG + FB)

Format inspired by a two-panel "do X, as if Y were watching" carousel (@yearlikeaghost, 21 Oct 2026). Our lines, character, scenes and ending are original; only the two-panel layout and warm golden-hour light are borrowed as a format.

- Hero: Momo (glass bulb head with warm inner glow, two black oval eyes, no mouth, gold screw neck, navy hoodie + joggers + gloves, white-navy sneakers).
- Look: warm golden-hour illustration, amber/orange light, soft shadows, cozy Indian home/street settings. 2D painted-comic look.
- Size: 1080×1350 (4:5), each slide = top panel + bottom panel (1080×675 each), thin divider.
- Text: English, white bold rounded sans with soft dark shadow, centred where the panels meet (top line sits low in the top panel, bottom line high in the bottom panel). No watermark.
- Images: ChatGPT (Suraj's Plus account) with momo_master.png as reference; text added in code afterwards (not baked into the image) so spelling and placement stay exact.
