  - Petrol ₹8.51 L · Diesel ₹5.77 L · CNG ₹4.80 L · EV (home) ₹3.58 L · EV (public DC only) ₹5.66 L

- Autocar's "who suits what": diesel = high mileage/long highway runs; EV = reliable home charging, urban use, high running; CNG = "sweet spot"; petrol = lowest entry price. CNG caveat: limited stations/queues.
