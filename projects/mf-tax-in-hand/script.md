## Rules (FY 2026-27, unchanged in Budget 2026; govt said no plan to scrap LTCG, Jul 2026)
Equity MF held > 12 months: LTCG 12.5% above Rs 1.25 lakh/FY exemption, + 4% cess (surcharge only at high incomes).
Held <= 12 months: STCG 20% (+cess). SIP: every instalment has its own 12-month clock (FIFO). Exit load often 1% within 1 year (fund-specific).
Sources: sahi.com Budget 2026 LTCG guide; taxfetchindia.com (21 Jul 2026); bajajfinserv LTCG FY 2026-27.

## Maths (5-yr-old SIP, Rs 3 lakh gain, all units > 12 months)
One go: (3,00,000 - 1,25,000) x 12.5% = 21,875 + 4% cess = Rs 22,750 tax.
Split 1.5 L gain in March + 1.5 L gain in April (two FYs): each FY (1,50,000 - 1,25,000) x 12.5% x 1.04 = 3,250 -> Rs 6,500 total. Saved Rs 16,250.
