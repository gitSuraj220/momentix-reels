# Indian GTA — what India's image costs (draft v1, 20 Nov 2026)

Angle (b): what this image costs India in tourism money. Our own visuals only; no footage from the reference reel.

## Script (Hinglish, ~30 s)
1. Ek AI wala "Indian GTA" video viral hai — gandi nadi, sadak pe kachra. Hassi aayi? Par iska bill India bharta hai.
2. 2025 mein Thailand mein 3.3 crore videshi tourist aaye. Sirf ek shehar, Dubai — 1.96 crore.
3. Aur poore India mein? Sirf 91 lakh. 💀
4. Duniya ke har 100 tourist mein se India ke hisse mein bas 1.
5. Aur ye 91 lakh log bhi ₹2.7 lakh crore de gaye. Socho 3 crore aate toh?
6. Ab duniya India ko aise videos se jaanegi… toh flight kahan book karegi? Thailand. 😬
7. Video exaggerated hai, maana. Par kachra fake nahi hai. Fix karoge ya meme banate rahoge?

## Facts used (verified)
- India FTAs 2025: 91.49 lakh, −8.1% vs 99.52 lakh (2024); forex US$31.69B, −9.5%; 1.31% of world arrivals, 1.68% of receipts — PolicyEdge summary of MoT India Tourism Statistics.
- Forex 2025: ₹2,73,638 crore (provisional) — MoT Annual Report 2025–26 via TravTalk (2 Mar 2026).
- Thailand 2025: 32,974,321 foreign tourists, ~1.53 trillion baht — Nation Thailand (6 Jan 2026).
- Dubai 2025: 19.59 million international overnight visitors — Dubai Media Office (9 Feb 2026).
