Duniya ki sabse hyped AI company ka CEO tension mein hai. Kyunki uska ChatGPT ghar-ghar pahunch gaya... phir bhi company loss mein ja rahi hai. Welcome to the AI era... jahan ek simple sa chatbot, dekhte-dekhte 7 trillion dollar ka gamble ban gaya.

30 November 2022. Budhwar ka din. Ek company mein sab normal chal raha tha. Log coffee pi rahe the, memes share kar rahe the, developers bugs fix kar rahe the. Aur usi din OpenAI ne apna chatbot drop kiya... ChatGPT. 24 ghante ke andar internet ko naya obsession mil gaya. Koi email likhwa raha tha, koi code fix karwa raha tha, aur koi apni breakup story. ChatGPT itna viral hua ki sirf 5 din mein 10 lakh users aa gaye. Aur 2 mahine mein 10 crore. Instagram ko 10 crore users tak pahunchne mein dhai saal lage the. Facebook ko saadhe chaar saal. ChatGPT ban gaya... history ka sabse fast growing app.
