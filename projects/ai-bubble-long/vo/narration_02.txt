Phir kya tha... Google ke headquarters mein full panic mode on ho gaya. Google full hustle mode mein aa gaya. Technology uske paas thi. Saalon se thi. Par company dar rahi thi... ki AI kahin kuch galat na bol de, koi case na ho jaye, media mein naam kharab na ho jaye. Aur OpenAI? Usne bina dare launch kar diya. Kuch hi hafton mein Microsoft ne OpenAI mein 10 billion dollar laga diye. Aur 3 billion dollar alag se ready rakhe. Google ne jaldi-jaldi mein Bard launch kiya... Aur bas... pehle hi din Gemini ne poora raita phaila diya.

Doosri taraf, Amazon ne Anthropic mein billions lagane shuru kar diye. Aur investors ka scene ye tha... Ek company ne toh aisa AI banane ke liye 3 crore dollar utha liye... jiska kaam tha doosre AI ke answers ko naye naam se wapas de dena.

Par asli pagalpan tha... GPU ki shortage. Computer ki ye chhoti-chhoti chips achanak sone aur tel se bhi zyada valuable ban gayi. Aur Nvidia duniya ki sabse valuable company ban gayi. Nvidia ke CEO Jensen Huang conferences mein leather jacket pehen ke aise aane lage... jaise koi rockstar ho. Aur ek tarah se... wo the bhi. Ek high-end GPU ka kiraya tha lagbhag 3 dollar per ghanta. Sasta lag raha hai na? Par startup ko ek GPU nahi, sainkdon chahiye hote hain. Sirf ek model train karne ke liye bhi ek chhota startup 500 chips kiraye pe le sakta hai. Yaani 1,500 dollar per ghanta. 36,000 dollar per din. Aur mahine ke 11 lakh dollar se zyada. Sirf GPU ke kiraye mein. Phir bhi companies GPU aise jama kar rahi thi... jaise duniya khatam hone wali ho aur log ration stock kar rahe hon. Nvidia jitni speed se chips bana rahi thi, demand usse bhi fast thi. Ek time pe waiting list 12 mahine tak pahunch gayi. Black market shuru ho gaya. Log ye chips border paar smuggle karne lage.
