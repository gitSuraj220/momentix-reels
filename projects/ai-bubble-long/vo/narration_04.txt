Phir gadbadiyan saamne aane lagi. ChatGPT "hallucinate" karne laga. Tech ki bhasha mein iska matlab hai... poore confidence ke saath kuch bhi bana ke bol dena. Aise research papers ka reference, jo exist hi nahi karte. Aise court cases, jo kabhi hue hi nahi. Aur aise numbers, jo shayad kisi doosre planet se aaye the. Google ke AI ne ek user ko salah di ki pizza pe cheese chipkane ke liye usme glue daal do. Ye usne Reddit ke ek mazaak se seekha tha. Haan, sach mein. Phir cases shuru hue. Writers, artists, journalists... sabka ek hi sawaal tha: "Kya tumne mere copyrighted kaam se apna AI train kiya hai?" Jawab? "Umm... haan." The New York Times ne OpenAI pe case kiya. Getty Images ne Stability AI pe case kiya. Writers ne companies ko court mein ghaseeta. Artists ne baaki companies ko. 2024 ke beech tak cracks dikhne lage the. Hype bahut loud tha. Par numbers darawne the. Aur ab sabke dimaag mein ek hi sawaal ghoom raha tha... bilkul waise, jaise raat ke 3 baje kaan ke paas machhar ghoomta hai: "Ye bubble phootega kab?"

Loss behisaab hai. Companies ki value reality se kahin zyada lagti hai. Lekin ye bubble waise nahi phoot raha, jaise dot-com ka bubble phoota tha. Kyun? Kyunki ise phulane walon ki jeb duniya mein sabse gehri hai. 1999 mein startups ki kamaai zero hoti thi... aur office mein masti ke liye 17 foosball tables hoti thi. Aaj AI bubble ko paisa dene wali badi tech companies sach mein kamaati hain. Bahut zyada. Itna paisa ki agar wo chahein... toh poora desh khareed lein. Microsoft, Azure se billions kamata hai. Google, ads se billions kamata hai. Meta aapko wahi meme dikha ke billions kamata hai... jo aapka cousin pehle hi WhatsApp pe bhej chuka hai. Ye badi companies AI mein isliye invest nahi kar rahi ki abhi isse profit ho raha hai. Wo isliye invest kar rahi hain... kyunki unhe darr hai ki kahin wo peeche na reh jayein. Agar AI agla iPhone moment ban gaya toh? Agla Google moment? Agar ye wo pal hua, jab poori duniya raaton-raat badal jaati hai? Toh jis company ne speed kam ki... wo agli BlackBerry ban jayegi. Aur koi bhi BlackBerry nahi banna chahta. In badi tech companies ko poker ke aise players samjho, jo unlimited paisa leke table pe baithe hain. Microsoft peeche hata, toh Google jeet jayega. Google peeche hata, toh Amazon jeet jayega. Amazon peeche hata, toh Meta jeet jayega. Koi palak nahi jhapka raha. Koi fold nahi kar raha. Har koi bet badhata ja raha hai.
