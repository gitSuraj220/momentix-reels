Aur yahin se asli tough sawaal aata hai. Agar har koi kharch karta rahe... toh bubble phootega kaise? Short answer hai: Jab real duniya ka koi event sabko majboor kar de... ki ab dikhawa band karna padega. History mein aise events aam taur pe do tarah ke hote hain. Pehla: achanak paisa milna band ho jaye. Dot-com ka bubble isliye nahi phoota tha ki website banana koi bekaar idea tha. Wo isliye phoota kyunki paise ka nal band ho gaya. Interest rates badhe. Loan mehenga ho gaya. Funding gayab ho gayi. Aur hazaaron companies raaton-raat khatam ho gayi. Agar aane wale time mein interest rates badhte hain aur badi tech companies apna budget tight kar deti hain... toh AI bubble ka game turant khatam. Doosra: koi bahut badi aur sharmnaak failure saamne aa jaye. Har bubble ka ek aisa moment hota hai. Ek itna bada fail, jise dekh ke sab ghabra jaate hain. Dot-com ke time company results ne ummeedein todi thi. 2008 mein Lehman Brothers tha. Crypto mein FTX tha. AI ke case mein ye kisi badi lab ke bhaari loss ka khulasa ho sakta hai. Kisi naye model ka buri tarah fail launch ho sakta hai. Ya koi aisa case, jo sab kuch rok de.

Toh abhi hum kahan khade hain? Badi tech companies ab bhi kharch kar rahi hain. Kisi result ne abhi tak bhoochaal nahi machaya. FTX level ka koi scam saamne nahi aaya. Cracks dikh rahe hain... par itne bade nahi ki bharosa toot jaye. Toh haan, ye bubble phoola hua hai. Haan, numbers pagal kar dene wale hain. Lekin ye sirf hype aur shor ki wajah se nahi phootega. Ye tab phootega, jab in giants mein se koi aakhirkar bolega... "Theek hai. Ab bahut ho gaya." Tab tak bubble hawa mein tairta rahega. Hum abhi explosion wale point pe nahi hain. Hum us point pe hain jahan tension lagataar badh rahi hai. Cheekh nikalne se pehle andar kheenchi gayi saans. Dor tootne se pehle ka khinchaav. Season ke last episode se theek pehle wala episode.
