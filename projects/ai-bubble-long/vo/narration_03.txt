2024 tak har cheez "AI-powered" ho chuki thi. Aapka toaster ab bread ko 10% zyada efficiently jala sakta tha. Aapka calendar app algorithm se meeting ka time suggest kar raha tha. Aur supermarket ka self-checkout? Camera toh pehle bhi tha... bas ab uska naam "machine learning" ho gaya tha. Party full on thi. Champagne khul rahi thi. Cheques clear ho rahe the. Phir kisi ne wo awkward sawaal pooch liya... "Bhai... ye sab chalane ka kharcha kitna aa raha hai?"

January 2025. Sam Altman ne ek aisa tweet kiya, jisne investors ka BP high kar diya. "OpenAI ke Pro subscription pe humein loss ho raha hai. Log ise humari expectation se kahin zyada use kar rahe hain." Haan, aapne sahi suna. OpenAI apne Pro plan ke 200 dollar mahina leta hai. Phir bhi har subscriber pe loss ho raha hai. Par ruko... story aur kharab hoti hai. 2024 mein OpenAI ka hisaab dekho. Revenue: 3.7 billion dollar. Loss: 5 billion dollar. Company sirf profit kamane mein fail nahi hui... jitna kamaya, usse zyada uda diya. Ab aata hai asli shocking part. Ek research firm ke hisaab se, 2030 tak AI aur uske data centers chalane ke liye 6.7 trillion dollar chahiye honge. Ise thoda tod ke samjhte hain. 5.2 trillion dollar seedha AI ke infrastructure pe: GPU, chips, bijli, cooling aur data center banane pe. Baaki 1.5 trillion dollar cloud infrastructure pe. Aur sirf chaar sabse badi tech companies se 2025 mein 350 billion dollar kharch karne ki ummeed thi. Ek saal mein. Is kharche ko justify karne ke liye AI companies ko 2030 tak har saal 2 trillion dollar kamane honge. Aur abhi poori AI industry ki total kamaai? Lagbhag 200 billion dollar. Yaani kamaai ko 10 guna karna hoga. 2021 se private investors AI startups mein sainkdon billion dollar daal chuke hain. Iska zyada hissa sirf 10 companies ko mila, jinki total value lagbhag 1 trillion dollar badh gayi. Har koi yahi bet laga raha tha... ki inmein se koi agla Google banega.
