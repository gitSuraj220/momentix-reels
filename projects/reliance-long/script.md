## 1. CHORWAD SE ADEN (0:25–1:20)
AMRUT: Gujarat ka ek chhota sa gaon, Chorwad. 1932 mein yahan Dhirajlal Hirachand Ambani paida hue. Pyaar se log kehte the... Dhirubhai.
Pita school teacher the. Ghar mein paisa kam tha.
Sirf 16–17 saal ki umar mein Dhirubhai Yemen ke Aden shehar chale gaye.
Wahan ek company, A. Besse, mein naukri mili. Kaam? Shell petrol pump aur tel ka kaam. Salary? Kareeb ₹300 mahina.
Par Dhirubhai ka dimaag naukri mein nahi, mauke mein tha.
Kehte hain, Yemen ke chaandi ke sikkon mein jitni chaandi thi, uski keemat sikke ki keemat se zyada thi. Dhirubhai ne sikke khareede, pighla ke chaandi bechi, aur munafa kamaya.
[PHOTO: Aden 1950s port] [ANIM: coin → silver bar, ₹ counter]

DEVI: Toh wo wahin bade aadmi ban gaye?

AMRUT: Nahi. 1958 mein wo India wapas aa gaye. Ek bada sapna leke.

## 2. ₹15,000 AUR EK CHHOTA KAMRA (1:20–2:05)
AMRUT: Mumbai, Masjid Bunder. Ek chhota sa office. Ek table, teen kursi.

Pehle masale bheje, phir kapde ka dhaaga, yaani polyester yarn.
Us time India mein polyester bahut mehenga aur kam milta tha. Dhirubhai ne yahin apna mauka dekha.

## 3. "ONLY VIMAL" (2:05–2:50)
AMRUT: Par trader ko hamesha doosre ka maal bechna padta hai. Dhirubhai khud maal banana chahte the.

Phir aaya brand... Vimal.
Wholesalers ne maal lene se mana kiya, toh Dhirubhai ne seedha logon tak pahunchne ka raasta banaya. Apni dukaanein, bade ads, aur ek line: "Only Vimal."

## 4. ₹10 KA SHARE (2:50–3:55)
AMRUT: Ab factory badi karni thi. Iske liye chahiye tha bahut saara paisa.
Bank? Bade seth? Dhirubhai ne teesra raasta chuna... aam log.
1977 mein Reliance ka IPO aaya. ₹10 ka share.
Issue saat guna oversubscribe hua. Pehle hi saal 58,000 log Reliance ke shareholder ban gaye.
Aur 1980 ke dashak mein ye ginti lakhon mein pahunch gayi.
Pehli baar chhote shehron ka teacher, clerk, dukaandaar... share market mein aaya. Isse kehte hain "equity cult."

## 5. DHAAGE SE TEL TAK (3:55–4:40)
AMRUT: Yahi Dhirubhai ki asli chaal thi. Ulta chalna. Backward integration.
Kapda banane ke liye chahiye dhaaga. Dhaage ke liye chahiye polyester. Polyester ke liye petrochemicals. Aur petrochemicals ke liye... crude oil.
Reliance ek-ek step peeche gayi, aur har step khud banaya.
Aden ke petrol pump wala ladka ab khud tel ka business khada kar raha tha.
[ANIM: chain kapda ← dhaaga ← polyester ← petrochem ← crude, each lights up]

## 6. JAMNAGAR (4:40–5:15)
AMRUT: Is sapne ka sabse bada hissa tha Jamnagar.
Gujarat mein ek refinery, jo 1999 mein shuru hui. Aaj ye duniya ki sabse badi single-location refinery complex hai.
Iske project ko lead kiya Dhirubhai ke bete, Mukesh Ambani ne.
