Corona mein thali bajayi thi... aur iss saal sarkar ne sabke phone baja diye. Yaad hai na? Korea ne bhi yahi kiya, par full smart way mein. Scene ye hai: Korea ke national tax exam ka topper khud bola, "Rank one maine AI se cheat karke liya." Chhupe camera se paper ki photo li, AI ko bheji, aur answer phone pe. Iske baad sarkar full alert pe aa gayi. Unnees November ko, jab lagbhag paanch lakh bachche Korea ka sabse bada college exam de rahe the, sarkar ne poore desh ke phones pe emergency alarm baja diya. Teen baar. Silent pe bhi. Jinhone phone jama nahi kiya tha, unka bag hi cheekh pada. Literally caught in four K... unchaas bachche pakde gaye. Ek ke paas toh mini camera aur wireless earphones bhi mile. Aur ab sarkar AI companies se exam wale din ka data maang rahi hai. India mein ye alarm abhi sirf disaster ke liye bajta hai...
