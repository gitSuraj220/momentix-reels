## Facts
- Making charges 8–25% (Business Standard, 30 Sep 2025); example 20%.
- GST 3% on gold value; making also taxed (sources say 3–5%). ₹10,000 at 20% making → gold value ≈ ₹8,100 (10,000 / (1.20 × 1.03…1.04) = ₹8,065–8,090); making ≈ ₹1,620; GST ≈ ₹290.
- Resale: making/wastage deducted → typical 10–15% loss (Business Standard).
- Digital gold: 3% GST + 2–3% spread → ~₹500–600 loss on ₹10,000 (Nithin Kamath via Business Today, Nov 2025); SEBI: outside its framework.
- Gold ETF: no GST on purchase; expense ratio 0.2–1%/yr + brokerage; SEBI-regulated (Business Standard; Bajaj AMC; Angel One).
