# LONG VIDEO script v1 (16:9, 5-6 min, Suraj's voice recorded in parts): "Where to Keep Money for 6 Months to 3 Years?"
Style ref: Meanwhile Mayank "Delhi Pollution Explained": notebook page, numbered chapters ("01 · ..."), paper-collage scenes, report cards.
Idea inspired by a Hindi YT video on short-term options; OUR OWN script, flow, examples and numbers.

## Facts (checked 7 Nov 2026)
- SBI FD (since 15 Dec 2025): 1-2 yr 6.25%, 2-3 yr 6.40%, 3-5 yr 6.30% (seniors +0.5%). Early-break penalty 0.5% (<5 L) / 1%. (Policybazaar)
- DICGC: Rs 5 lakh per bank per depositor (all deposits together); govt considering a hike, not yet in force per sources. NBFC deposits not covered.
- Liquid/arbitrage funds: historically ~6-7.5%/yr, varies (Jainam, May 2026). Liquid = slab tax on exit; arbitrage = equity tax (20% <12 m; 12.5% >12 m above Rs 1.25 L/FY).
- Example (Rs 3 L, 3 yrs, 30% slab + cess, same 6.5%/6.3% assumptions): FD ~Rs 3.42 L in hand; liquid ~Rs 3.43 L; arbitrage ~Rs 3.62 L (if no other LTCG that year). Example only.

## PART 1 · Hook (~25 s)
Bunty ne Goa trip ke liye ₹3 lakh bachaaye… aur sabse "safe" jagah daal diye — FD mein.
August mein ek zabardast Goa package mila. Par paisa? FD mein lock. Todta toh penalty lagti.
Toh sawaal ye hai: jo paisa 6 mahine se 3 saal mein chahiye, use rakhein kahan?
Aaj FD se lekar liquid fund aur arbitrage fund tak, saare options simple bhasha mein samjhenge.

## PART 2 · 3 cheezein pehle (~35 s)
Kisi bhi option ko teen cheezon pe check karo.
Pehla, time: aapko paisa kab chahiye? 6 mahine, 1 saal, ya 3 saal.
Doosra, risk: zero risk kuch nahi hota, bas kam ya zyada hota hai.
Teesra, liquidity: zarurat pade toh paisa kitni jaldi aur kitni aasaani se nikle.
Ye teen meter poori video mein har option ke saath dikhenge.

## PART 3 · Savings account (~20 s)
Sabse aasaan: savings account. Paisa kabhi bhi nikaal lo.
Par byaaj sirf 2.5 se 3%, aur mehngai usse zyada. Matlab paisa pada pada chhota hota jaata hai.

## PART 4 · FD: 4 tarah ki (~60 s)
FD sab jaante hain, par FD chaar tarah ki hoti hai.
Sarkari bank: sabse bharosemand, isliye byaaj thoda kam. Jaise SBI abhi 1 se 3 saal pe lagbhag 6.3%.
Private bank: competition ke liye thoda zyada byaaj dete hain.
Small finance bank: jo un logon ko loan dete hain jinhe aasaani se loan nahi milta. Risk zyada, isliye FD rate bhi zyada.
Aur NBFC: ye bank nahi, finance company hai. Rate sabse attractive, par risk bhi sabse zyada.
Ek zaroori baat: bank mein aapka paisa ₹5 lakh tak insured hota hai. Savings, FD, RD sab mila ke, har bank mein. Par NBFC FD pe ye insurance nahi milta.

## PART 5 · FD ka asli kharcha: tax (~45 s)
FD ke fayde: pakka byaaj, pata hai kab milega, phone pe 2 minute mein khul jaati hai.
Par do nuksaan. Pehla, rate lock: baad mein rates badhe toh aapko fayda nahi.
Doosra, aur sabse bada: tax. FD ka byaaj har saal aapki income mein judta hai, aur slab ke hisaab se tax lagta hai. Paisa nikaalo ya na nikaalo.
30% slab mein ho, toh 6.3% ka byaaj asal mein reh jaata hai lagbhag 4.3%.

## PART 6 · Liquid fund (~40 s)
Ab liquid fund. Ye aapka paisa companies aur sarkar ko sirf 91 din tak ke liye udhaar deta hai.
Jaise Diwali se pehle ek dukaan ko maal bharne ke liye kuch hafton ka paisa chahiye. Wo lete hain, kamaate hain, lauta dete hain.
Time chhota, isliye risk kam. Aur paisa lagbhag ek din mein wapas.
Tax? Wahi slab rate. Par sirf tab, jab aap paisa nikaaloge. Tab tak poora paisa badhta rehta hai.

## PART 7 · Arbitrage fund (~45 s)
Aur ab sabse interesting: arbitrage fund.
Maan lo ek hi company ka share NSE pe ₹100 ka hai aur BSE pe ₹102 ka. Fund sasta khareedta hai, mehenga bechta hai, aur beech ka ₹2 kamaata hai. Aisa roz hazaaron baar.
Risk kam, kyunki bet market ke upar-neeche pe nahi, sirf price ke gap pe hai.
Aur twist: ise tax equity ki tarah lagta hai. Saal se pehle becha toh 20%. Saal ke baad becha toh 12.5%, wo bhi ₹1.25 lakh ke profit ke upar.
Isliye 30% slab walon ke liye ye bada farak bana sakta hai.

## PART 8 · Debt funds: low aur short duration (~35 s)
Teesra option: debt funds. Inme do kaam ke hain.
Low duration fund 6 se 12 mahine ke papers mein paisa lagata hai. Short duration fund 1 se 3 saal ke.
Simple rule: jitne time baad paisa chahiye, usi duration ka fund.
Tax? Liquid fund jaisa, aapke slab pe, nikaalne pe.

## PART 9 · ₹3 lakh, 3 saal: kisme kitna haath mein? (~40 s)
Ab Bunty ka ₹3 lakh, 3 saal, aur Bunty 30% slab mein hai.
FD 6.3% pe: haath mein lagbhag ₹3.42 lakh.
Liquid fund 6.5% pe: lagbhag ₹3.43 lakh. FD jaisa hi, bas tax baad mein.
Arbitrage fund 6.5% pe: lagbhag ₹3.62 lakh, kyunki tax kaafi kam laga.
Matlab ek hi paisa, ek hi time… par galat jagah rakha toh ₹20,000 tak ka farak.
(Ye sirf example hai. Fund ke returns badalte rehte hain, guaranteed nahi.)

## PART 10 · Kaise chunein + end (~30 s)
Toh shortcut yaad rakho.
Paisa kabhi bhi chahiye? Savings ya liquid fund.
6 se 12 mahine? FD, liquid ya low duration.
1 saal se zyada aur high tax slab? Arbitrage ya short duration bhi dekh sakte ho.
Aur sabse zaroori: apna time, risk aur liquidity pehle tay karo, phir option.
