# ₹1 = $1 ho gaya toh? — long video, draft v1 (21 Nov 2026)

Format: 16:9, 1920×1080, ~5.5–6 min, Hinglish VO (Suraj's clone). Angle: "your pocket" — every point shown on the
Verma family + a live ₹ calculator on screen. Reference: Plain Paisa long video (same topic; our own words, data, visuals).
Family: Papa (Rajesh, works in Dubai) · Mummy (Sunita, runs the home budget) · Didi (Priya, IT job, US clients) · Chhotu (Aarav, dreams of a US college).

## Script

**1. Hook (0:00)**
Socho, kal subah uthte hi ₹1 = $1. ₹1,32,900 wala iPhone 17 Pro… lagbhag ₹1,100 mein! Dubai trip? Kuch sau rupaye. America ki college? Saal ke sirf ₹50,000.
Maza aa gaya na? Ruko. Ek hafte mein ye sapna India ki economy hila dega. Aaj Verma family ke saath dekhte hain — ye "sasta dollar" unki jeb ke saath kya karta hai.

**2. Aaj ka rate (0:25)**
Aaj ek dollar lagbhag ₹93 ka hai. Is saal May mein toh ₹96.9 tak gaya tha — ab tak ka sabse neeche.
Toh sarkar ek kanoon bana de: "Kal se ₹1 = $1." Kya hoga?

**3. Din 1: dollar gayab (0:45)**
Subah hote hi poora desh bank pahunchega: "Mujhe dollar do, ek rupaye ka ek!" Kyun nahi, itna sasta jo hai.
RBI ke paas lagbhag $720 billion hain. Bahut lagta hai na? Par ₹1 = $1 pe ye sirf ₹72,000 crore ban jaata hai. Crores log ek saath maangenge… dopahar tak khatam.
Phir dollar milega black market mein — asli rate pe, ya usse bhi mehenga. Dukaandar bhi wahi se kharidega. Matlab iPhone sasta hua hi nahi. Sasta dollar sirf unko milega jinki "setting" hai.

**4. Papa, Dubai (1:30)**
Rajesh ji Dubai mein kaam karte hain, har mahine 3,000 dirham ghar bhejte hain. Aaj woh lagbhag ₹76,000 bante hain.
Kal? Sirf ₹817. 😶
Par ghar ki EMI, Chhotu ki school fees, Mummy ka kitchen — sab utne ke utne. Aur ye sirf ek ghar nahi: bahar kaam karne wale Indians ne pichhle saal $135 billion se zyada ghar bheja — duniya mein sabse zyada.

**5. Didi, IT job (2:10)**
Priya Bengaluru mein IT job karti hai. Uski company America ke clients se dollar mein kamaati hai.
Aaj Priya ki ₹1 lakh salary ke liye client ko lagbhag $1,075 dene padte hain. ₹1 = $1 pe? Poore $1,00,000.
93 guna zyada kaun dega? Koi nahi. Kaam dusre desh chala jaayega… aur Priya jaisi lakhon naukriyan khatre mein.

**6. Chhotu, US college (2:45)**
Aur Chhotu ka sapna? America ki $50,000 wali college aaj lagbhag ₹46.6 lakh ki hai. Kagaz pe kal sirf ₹50,000.
Par dollar milega kahan? Bank mein khatam, black market mein wahi purana rate. Sasta sirf kagaz pe.

**7. Ye theory nahi hai (3:05)**
Zimbabwe ne 2016 mein naye note nikaale aur bola: ye US dollar ke barabar hain. Kuch hi samay mein bazaar ne unhe dollar se kaafi kam pe lena shuru kar diya.
Kanoon se rate likh sakte ho. Bharosa nahi.

**8. WhatsApp wala myth (3:30)**
"1947 mein ₹1 = $1 tha" — ye forward aapne dekha hoga. Galat hai. 1947 mein ek dollar lagbhag ₹3.30 ka tha.
Toh ₹3 se ₹93 tak kaise pahunche?

**9. Asli wajah: Mummy ka kitchen + petrol (3:55)**
Pehli wajah — mehngai. 1990 se India mein cheezon ke daam lagbhag 10 guna badhe, America mein sirf 2.5 guna. Jab rupaya ghar mein kam khareedta hai, duniya bhi usse kam value deti hai.
Doosri — tel. India apna lagbhag 88% crude bahar se khareedta hai, dollar mein. Tel mehenga = zyada dollar chahiye = rupaya neeche.
Aur jhatke: 1991 mein India ke dollar lagbhag khatam ho gaye — 47 ton sona girvi rakhna pada. Is saal West Asia ki jung se tel $100 ke paar gaya, foreign investors ne ₹2 lakh crore se zyada nikaale — aur May mein rupaya ₹96.9.

**10. Big reveal: number se kuch nahi hota (4:45)**
Ek dollar mein lagbhag 158 Japanese yen aate hain — rupaye se bhi zyada. Phir bhi Japan duniya ke sabse amir deshon mein hai.
Aur ₹1 = $1 karne ka ek legal tareeka bhi hai: Turkey ne 2005 mein apni currency se 6 zero hata diye. Daam chhote, salary chhoti — badla kuch nahi.
India bhi bol sakta hai "93 purane rupaye = 1 naya rupaya". Boom, ₹1 = $1. Par Priya ki ₹1 lakh salary bhi ban jaayegi lagbhag ₹1,075.

**11. Rupaya strong kaise banta hai (5:15)**
Strong currency kanoon se nahi, demand se banti hai. Duniya ke 89% currency trades mein dollar hota hai — kyunki duniya ko us pe bharosa hai.
Rupaya strong hoga jab: duniya zyada Made in India khareede, hum kam tel import karein — EV, solar —, mehngai control mein rahe, aur duniya ko hum pe bharosa ho.

**12. Ending (5:45)**
Toh ₹1 = $1 ka sapna? Kanoon se nahi milega — kamaana padega.
Tumhare ghar pe rupaye ka sabse bada asar kahan hai — petrol, phone, ya bahar ki padhai? Comment mein batao.
Sirf jaankari ke liye.
