📌 Is short mein:
✅ IPO kya hai: company public se paisa leti hai (bank loan ki jagah)
✅ IPO process 4 steps: DRHP (SEBI ko filing) → Price band → Bidding → Listing
✅ Company ko paisa issue price pe milta hai — listing gain company ko nahi, allottees ko jaata hai
✅ Real example: Jio Platforms IPO — issue ₹1,119, listing ₹1,358 (+21.36%), ₹30,213 crore fresh issue
