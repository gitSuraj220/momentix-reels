# Findings (hypotheses; status changes only when 2+ videos agree)

1. **Live money decision the viewer is making THIS WEEK wins.** Jio IPO allotment-lottery (1.07M views, 13.9k shares) and Fortuner tax (76k) both told people something about money they are spending right now, with a "you think X, actually Y" twist. Status: supported (3 videos: Jio allotment 1.12M, Jio listing tax 262k, Fortuner 87k).
2. **Hook = a surprising number about the viewer's own money** ("10 lot lagaye, phir bhi 0 share?", "₹40 lakh mein kitna tax?"). Supported (2).
3. **Generic how-it-works tech/health without a money or "you" stake flops** (earphones, wireless charging, Starlink, vaccine, smartwatch: 200–320 views, 3s rate <45%). Supported (5).
4. **Political topics flop** (voter list, jammer: <200 views). Supported (2).
5. **Same topic repeats only with a personal stake**: Jio listing-history list (25 Oct) 2k, but Jio listing-gain *tax on your profit* (27 Oct) 262k. The topic isn't the driver; "your money, this week" is. Supported (2).
6. **Shares drive reach**: viral reel's share count (13.9k) > saves (5.7k). Write for "send this to the friend who…". Open.
7. **Generic money-habit advice flops** (Money Arc 4 habits: 692 views, 2.7 s avg, 22% 3s rate; dopamine/savings: 897, 7.9 s). Advice with no live event and no surprising number loses the first 3 s. Supported (2).
8. **Baseline lifted after the viral reels**: non-viral reels went from ~100-300 views (11-17 Oct) to ~1.5-3k (18-30 Oct). Followers 4 → 629, but daily follower gain has decayed to single digits by 29 Oct, so the bump needs a new "live decision" hit to hold. Open.
